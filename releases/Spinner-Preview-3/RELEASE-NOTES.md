# X16 Pinball Spinner Preview 3

- Up Arrow nudges the table. Three accepted presses within three seconds
  trigger tilt. The upper red X flashes, points stop accumulating, and
  flippers stay inactive until the next ball. A held key counts once.
- Clear the first three drop targets to earn a four-second spinner boost.
  The award rises from 500 to 1,500 points per step.
- The upper panel shows tilt, spinner boost, or a wormhole visit. The lower
  panel retains the active mission and its countdown through a capture.

Both editions come in 512 KB and 2 MB ZIPs. Extract your ZIP to a new folder
with x16emu.exe and its matching rom.bin in the parent folder. Run
RUN-WINDOWS.bat. The Windows launcher has yet to be tested on Windows.

See VALIDATION.json for the 8 MHz emulator runs and timing results. The
133,333-cycle budget remains open. Skill shots, wider lamp coverage, the
physical center drain blocker, and advanced missions remain open.
