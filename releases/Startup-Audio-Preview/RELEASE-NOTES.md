# X16 Pinball Startup Audio Preview

The lamp show now plays a newly synthesized electronic cue on cold start
and whenever F2 starts a new game. Two alternating pulses mark the opening
and every other lamp phase. The pulses stop as the message panes come up.
The existing ball-feed sound plays when the plunger becomes available.

F2 during the show skips to the serve. F3 leaves the show for the demo.
The cue uses the effects mixer, so it still plays with music muted.
The 512 KB and 2 MB soundtracks and the calibrated 2 MB mix are carried
over from the Startup Preview.

Each ZIP includes the runtime, its startup sound bank and RUN-WINDOWS.bat.
Put x16emu.exe and its matching rom.bin in the parent folder.

The cue follows the timing of the supplied enhanced Windows capture.
Its waveform is newly synthesized for X16. See VALIDATION.json for Linux
emulator evidence. Windows launcher execution and extended frame timing
remain open.
