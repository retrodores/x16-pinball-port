# Pinball releases and development archive

The [Space CmdrX16 2D Pinball 1.0 release](https://github.com/retrodores/x16-pinball-port/releases/tag/v1.0) offers the standard
512 KB and 2 MB builds and the Christmas 2 MB build. Versioned packages,
notes and checksums are in [1.0](1.0/).

## Space Cadet X16 exemplar

The archived exemplar shows the reference presentation on the same X16
engine. Its stock 8 MHz 65C02 ZIPs live here in the repository.

- [512 KB exemplar](1.0/exemplar/SpaceCadet-X16-1.0-Exemplar-512KB.zip)
- [2 MB exemplar](1.0/exemplar/SpaceCadet-X16-1.0-Exemplar-2MB.zip)
- [Exemplar checksums](1.0/exemplar/SHA256SUMS)

See the [build handoff](../docs/release-status/v1.0/HANDOFF.md) for the
standard, Christmas and exemplar build commands. Older previews and their
validation are preserved under the other folders in this directory.
